OStudy Tutor LMS Ads 1.7.0

NEW IN 1.7.0
- [ostudy_advertise]: one "Advertise with us" page (business ad order form, course/tutor sponsorship plans, order tracking).
  Open a tab directly with ?ad_tab=order | sponsor | track.
- Fixed prices (Settings > Pricing): price per week per placement, long-booking discounts and a currency label. Live total in the
  form; "Amount due" is filled automatically (still editable per order).
- Slot limits: max ads per placement at the same time. Awaiting payment, Under review and Live orders reserve a slot.
- Image rules: min 60% of the recommended width and a matching shape (browser + server). Can be switched off in Settings.
- Renewals and reminders: "Renew this ad" on the tracker, e-mails 7 and 3 days before the end, automatic completion (daily job).
- Setup check and test email on Ads > Orders.
